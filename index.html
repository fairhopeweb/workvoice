WV Install WorkVoice on iOS

WorkVoice installs as a full-screen app on your home screen — icon, offline shell, no browser chrome.

1
Open this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
2
Tap the Share button ⬆︎ in the toolbar.
3
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
4
Tap Add — launch WorkVoice from your home screen.
iOS only allows installs through Safari's share sheet — no one-tap install API exists. On Android/desktop Chrome, you may see a native install prompt instead.
